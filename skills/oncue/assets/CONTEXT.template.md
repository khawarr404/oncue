# Context for live answers

Your AI agent reads this before suggesting answers in your meetings. Keep it short and factual: answers are only
as good as what is written here. Update it whenever your project changes.

## About me
- Name, role, what I own in this project.

## People in my meetings
- Name: role, what they care about, questions they usually ask.

## The project in brief
- What it is, the current status, the method or plan in a few steps.

## Key numbers and facts
- Only numbers you are happy to say out loud.

## Known weak spots (and my honest answer)
- Weak spot: short answer.

## Links
- Main doc, demo, files.
