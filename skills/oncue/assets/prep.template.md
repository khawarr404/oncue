# Prep for this call

Written before the call from the user's own material (CV, notes, job ad or agenda) and public research.
Read during the call for answers. Keep it short; everything here must be true for the user.

## The call
- Type (interview, sales, client, standup, negotiation, meeting), date and time, length.
- With: name, role, what they care about (one line each).
- Goal: what a good outcome looks like for the user.

## Key facts
- The company or team: what they do, size, recent news (with the source), products, how they talk.
- Facts from the job ad or agenda that matter for this call.
- Sources not checked (no web access, paywalled): say so here.

## Likely questions and my answers
- Q: likely question
  A: one or two sentences, from the user's real experience. Behavioural: S / T / A / R, one line each.

## Questions to ask them
- Two to five good questions, tied to their situation.

## My numbers and limits
- Only figures the user gave (targets, ranges, prices, dates). Leave empty if none.

## Watch-outs
- Weak spots and the user's honest answer; topics to avoid.
