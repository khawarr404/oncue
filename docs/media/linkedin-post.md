Ever known the answer, but not in the 3 seconds before the pause gets awkward? 😅

So I built oncue 🎯 and today it's open source.

A live copilot for meetings, interviews and client calls that runs inside the AI agent you already use: Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, Copilot or Windsurf.

🎙️ Transcribes your call locally. No bot joins, no audio uploaded.
👂 Spots questions meant for you and ignores the chatter.
💡 Shows a short answer from your own notes. Never invents numbers.
⚡ One-tap Clarify, Recap and Follow-up, plus modes for interviews, sales and stand-ups.
📝 Writes your notes and actions when you hang up.

🔒 Local-first: with Ollama, everything stays on your machine.

Linux today 🐧 and I'd love help with macOS and Windows.
